About the Book

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly transforming our world. From personalized recommendations to automated decision-making systems, AI influences how we live, work, and interact with technology. This e-book explores both the promise and the ethical challenges of artificial intelligence. Through philosophy, real-world case studies, and thoughtful reflection, it examines how society can balance innovation with responsibility and human dignity.

What You'll Learn

🏠 AI at Home

How algorithms shape everyday life through recommendations, smart devices, and data collection.

💼 AI at Work

How automation improves productivity while raising concerns about job displacement.

⚖️ AI Ethics

Why fairness, transparency, and human oversight must guide AI development.

🚀 The Future of AI

How society can move forward responsibly with technology that serves humanity.
