About the Book

Artificial Intelligence is transforming daily life—from the way we communicate to the way businesses operate. This e-book explores both the promise and the ethical challenges of AI, examining how technology shapes privacy, work, morality, and the future of humanity.

Through philosophy, real-world case studies, and ethical reflection, this book encourages readers to think critically about the role AI should play in society.

What You'll Learn

AI in Everyday Life

How algorithms shape the information we see, the media we consume, and the choices we make.

AI in the Workplace

How automation is increasing productivity while also raising concerns about job displacement.

Ethics and Responsibility

Why fairness, transparency, and accountability must guide AI development.

The Future of AI

How society can balance innovation with human dignity and moral responsibility.

About the Author

Samarria Ladson is a reflective thinker with a passion for philosophy, ethics, and modern technology. Her work explores how innovation influences human identity and society through the lens of reason, faith, and ethical responsibility.
